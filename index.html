Why Me?

What sets my design and writing services apart.

1. Do you offer both Graphic Design and Resume Writing?

Yes! I have dual expertise. I can create stunning visual designs for your brand and simultaneously craft highly professional, ATS-optimized resumes and cover letters for your career advancement.

2. Why are your resumes specifically targeted at UK and USA markets?

The UK and USA markets have strict corporate standards for resumes (no photos, specific keyword densities, clean formatting). I specialize in these rules so your resume easily passes Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) in these competitive regions.

3. What kind of Graphic Design do you do?

I specialize in Flyer and Poster design, Social Media Creatives, and complete Brand Identity including logos. My focus is on creating high-end, premium visuals that immediately grab attention.

4. How many revisions do you offer?

Client satisfaction is my number one priority. I offer multiple revisions on both design projects and resume writing until we achieve a 100% perfect result that you are happy with.

5. How can I get started on a project?

Simply click the "Let's Talk" button or use the WhatsApp icon in the bottom right corner to send me a direct message! We can discuss your requirements and timeline immediately.
